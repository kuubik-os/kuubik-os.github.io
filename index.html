Minimal atomic OS with KDE Plasma, based on Fedora Kinoite.

Images

  • kuubik: for AMD and Intel GPUs.
  • kuubik-nvidia: for NVIDIA GPUs.

Features

  • CachyOS kernel instead of Fedora kernel.
  • NVIDIA drivers from negativo17 in kuubik-nvidia.
  • Multimedia codecs from RPM Fusion.
  • Less bloat: LibreOffice, Firefox, KDE games and other apps removed. Get what you need from Flathub.
  • Flathub and Homebrew ready to use.
  • Gaming: Steam and latest proton-cachyos.
  • VMs: vm-curator with QEMU/KVM.
  • Extras: distrobox, tailscale (off by default, turn on with sudo systemctl enable --now tailscaled).
  • Manual updates: nothing updates in the background. You get a reminder when the system is over a month old.
  • Secure boot support.
  • kctl: one command to update and manage the system.
